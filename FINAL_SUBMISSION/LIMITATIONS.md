# Limitations

1. The baseline action score is a heuristic decision-support approach rather than a complete production decision system.
2. The decline label is a proxy for content-performance behavior and does not by itself explain why content declined.
3. Historical performance signals may not capture every factor affecting search visibility.
4. Evaluation depends on the quality and coverage of the underlying warehouse data.
5. The workflow should be further validated before being used for automated business decisions.
6. A limited development period may not capture all seasonal or long-term content behavior.
7. Model and action recommendations should be interpreted as decision support, not as claims about the underlying search-engine algorithm.
